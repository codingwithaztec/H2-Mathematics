## Reflection

Parametric differentiation is relatively straightforward conceptually, once the relationship between dy/dx, dy/dt and dx/dt is understood.

However, the questions tend to involve more steps and algebraic manipulation than implicit differentiation. This makes it easier to overlook a differentiation step, lose a factor or make a mistake during simplification.

Overall, I found the topic manageable, with the main challenge being accuracy and keeping track of the multiple steps involved.
