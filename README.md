# H2 Mathematics — Independent Study

## About

This repository documents my independent study of the Singapore-Cambridge GCE A-Level H2 Mathematics syllabus (9758).

I am undertaking this journey to strengthen my mathematical foundations ahead of studying Applied AI & Analytics at Singapore Polytechnic, while preparing for further studies in mathematics, artificial intelligence and data science.

The repository serves as a record of my learning progress, containing mathematical notes, worked solutions, practice questions and reflections on topics covered.

## Objectives

- Develop a strong understanding of H2 Mathematics concepts.
- Strengthen problem-solving and mathematical reasoning skills.
- Work through challenging questions and examination-style problems.
- Build a foundation for further studies in mathematics and AI.
- Maintain a documented record of my independent learning journey.
